Parent Volunteer Engagement Programme

Stronger Together, Better Tomorrow

A shared commitment to enrich learning, inspire growth and build a stronger school community.

At our school, we firmly believe that education flourishes when it is nurtured through a strong and meaningful partnership between parents and educators. In alignment with the vision of NEP 2020 and the CBSE framework for holistic, experiential, and competency-based learning, we are pleased to introduce the Parent Volunteer Engagement Programme as a structured initiative to actively involve parents in the learning ecosystem.

This initiative is designed to move beyond traditional parent participation and create purposeful opportunities for parents to contribute their time, expertise, and experiences in ways that directly enrich student learning. We recognize that every parent brings a unique professional background, life experience, and skill set that can add immense value to our school community.

Through this programme, parents can engage in diverse roles such as supporting school events, mentoring students, conducting workshops, contributing to career guidance, assisting in experiential learning projects, and sharing real-world insights across various domains including academics, arts, technology, life skills, and more.

The objective of this initiative is to provide students with authentic exposure to real-world perspectives, enhance their confidence and curiosity, and strengthen their preparedness for future challenges. At the same time, it creates a collaborative platform where parents become active partners in shaping a vibrant and future-ready learning environment.

How Can You Contribute?

There are many ways to take part — choose the one that fits your expertise, your interest and the time you can give.

  • School Events & Celebrations

    Be part of events and experiences that bring our school community together.

  • Competitions & Exhibitions

    Share your expertise as a judge, mentor or facilitator.

  • Career Guidance & Mentoring

    Inspire senior students through your professional journey, experiences and insights.

  • Expert Talks & Workshops

    Share your knowledge and expertise through meaningful student learning experiences.

  • Experiential Learning & Projects

    Help students connect classroom learning with real-world contexts through visits, projects and authentic experiences.

  • Skills & Life Learning

    Facilitate sessions in areas such as arts, technology, entrepreneurship, life skills and other areas of expertise.
