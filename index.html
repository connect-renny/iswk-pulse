Strengthening the Home–School Partnership

At Indian School Al Wadi Al Kabir (ISWK), we believe that every child flourishes when home and school work together with trust, empathy and shared purpose.

PULSE is our dedicated parent engagement platform, thoughtfully aligned with the CBSE Parenting Calendar Framework and inspired by the vision of NEP 2020. It brings together meaningful opportunities for parents to stay informed, learn, connect, participate and access support throughout their child’s educational journey.

From Balvatika to Class XII, PULSE offers age-appropriate guidance and engagement through:

  • Parent Connect & Skill Builder Sessions

  • Parent–Teacher Meetings & Engagement Opportunities

  • Counselling, Wellbeing & Inclusive Education Support

  • Parent Volunteer & Parent–Child Engagement Programmes

  • Parenting Resources, Articles, Videos & Podcasts

  • Career Guidance, Digital Wellness & Life-Skills Support

  • Parent Voice, Feedback & Meaningful Conversations

The Parenting Calendar is designed around the evolving academic, social, emotional and developmental needs of children at every stage, empowering parents to become confident partners in their child’s growth.

A message of Vision & Mission

Dear Parents,

Every child’s journey is shaped not only by what they learn in classrooms, but also by the love, encouragement, values, and support they receive at home. At Indian School Al Wadi Al Kabir, we deeply believe that when parents and educators walk together with trust, understanding, and shared purpose, children flourish with confidence, happiness, and strength.

The ISWK Parenting Calendar 2026–2027 is born from this belief — a heartfelt effort to strengthen the precious partnership between home and school, and to nurture every learner holistically from Nursery to Class XII.

Inspired by the CBSE Parenting Calendar framework and thoughtfully customized for the ISWK community, this initiative goes beyond schedules and activities. It is a commitment to creating meaningful spaces for connection, reflection, collaboration, and shared growth. Through Parent Connect programmes, workshops, counselling initiatives, inclusive education support, and engagement activities, we hope to empower parents as valued partners in every child’s learning and life journey.

Together, we inspire. Together, we grow. Together, we make an impact.

Best Wishes

D N Rao
(Principal)
